# Power BI Dashboard for Tracking DT Vandalization(https://app.powerbi.com/links/DGreuj1wA9?ctid=581816b2-c0be-4eaa-80e3-29e5f2486494&pbi_source=linkShare)
## Overview and Purpose:
The dashboard effectively serves as an operational monitoring tool for tracking incidents affecting Distribution Transformers (DTs) vandalization across Ikeja Electric's service areas. This is a crucial application for an electric utility, as DTs are critical infrastructure components that transform voltage levels for distribution to end customers. Tracking vandalization incidents at these points helps prioritize maintenance, understand failure patterns, and improve service reliability.

## Dashboard Component Analysis:

1. Excellent geographical visualization showing the distribution of incidents across Lagos and surrounding areas.
2. Clear color coding (red for ongoing, green for resolved) provides immediate status visibility.
3. Clustering of markers helps identify hotspot areas requiring attention.



4. Clear presentation of total incident count (18).
5. The donut chart effectively shows the proportion of resolved vs. ongoing incidents.
6. Simple visualization that provides immediate understanding of resolution progress.


7. The bar chart clearly shows incident distribution across business units.
8. Color segmentation distinguishes between ongoing and resolved incidents.
9. Enables quick identification of units with the highest incident counts.
## Observations:

Ikorodu appears to have the highest number of total incidents
Some units like Oshodi show better resolution rates than others
Ikeja BU has the lowest incident count among the displayed units

## Temporal Analysis:

The line chart effectively shows incident trends over time
Day of week analysis helps identify patterns for resource planning
Visual presentation makes spike identification straightforward
Observations:

Notable incident spikes appear around January 28 and February 19
Tuesday and Wednesday show the highest incident counts
Weekend days (particularly Sunday) show lower incident frequencies


1. Multiple filter options including business units and transformer capacity.
2. Date range slider provides temporal filtering flexibility.
3. Capacity filters (200KVA, 300KVA, etc.) allow for equipment-specific analysis.

This dashboard provides valuable operational visibility across Ikeja Electric's distribution network. The combination of geographical, temporal, and organizational views creates a comprehensive monitoring tool for incident management.
